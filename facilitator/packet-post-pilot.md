Obtain the staff trial packet from the maintainer. It contains separate
question pages and answer keys; provide only the assigned question at the
time it is used. The local prepared packet is under
`work/packet-post-readiness/staff/` and is excluded from the student bundle.
Its authored keys still need independent human approval. Do not substitute
course A/B cases or finite reassessment variants.

Start with one beginner and one experienced learner, then revise material
findings before observing the remaining two to four participants. Record
versions and keep results from materially different versions separate.
Retesting a known item is supported practice, not unseen transfer.

Suggested routing session: 5 minutes for baseline, 15-20 for play,
5 for the unseen explanation, and 5 for feedback. Prerequisite instruction
and setup occur separately and must be recorded. Accommodate the learner's
needs without treating a time target as an exam deadline.
